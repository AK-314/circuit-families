Stage 14 random-label training trajectories for model seed 0 and random-label seed 1. The upper panel shows training and test accuracy at all 182 saved evaluations from step 0 through step 9,050. The horizontal dashed line marks the prespecified 0.99 memorisation threshold, and the dotted line marks chance accuracy (1/113). The lower panel shows training and test cross-entropy on a logarithmic scale. Vertical guides mark the seven exact main-model reference steps used for checkpoint matching: 200, 3,400, 7,450, 8,150, 8,500, 8,650 and 9,050. The random-label model reached at least 99% training accuracy by step 200 while test accuracy remained close to chance. The run therefore qualifies as a memorisation control; no sparse or diversity search is represented in this figure.
